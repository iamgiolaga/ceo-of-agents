# Migration 0.1.1 to 0.2.0

Version 0.2.0 moves `org.yaml` from schema `org/1` to `org/2`. Three things are added: `org.purpose` (what the fleet is for: `job`, `product`, `startup` or `company`), `cos.display_name` (the chief of staff's name) and the optional `employer_rules` key (a pointer for job hqs). The purpose drives suggested departments, the account rule and holding eligibility; the CoS name makes the CoS addressable like any department.

## Defaults

- `org.purpose: company` for every existing hq, holdings included. `company` is the only value whose rules equal the 0.1.1 rules (employer accounts excluded, holding-eligible, escalation available), so the migration changes no behaviour; for a holding it is the only valid value. The CoS shows the value in the diff. In the same review the CEO may pick `product` or `startup` (only the value changes) or `job` (then apply the extra edits in "Choosing job").
- `cos.display_name`: the name the CoS already uses in `CLAUDE.md` (the intro line "You are **<name>**, the chief of staff"), else `Chief of staff`. The CEO confirms it in the review.

## Edits

`org.yaml`:

```yaml
# before
schema: org/1
framework_version: 0.1.1
org:
  level: <level>
ceo:
  role_id: ceo
  display_name: "<name>"
policies_repo: <value>

# after
schema: org/2
framework_version: 0.2.0
org:
  level: <level>
  purpose: company
ceo:
  role_id: ceo
  display_name: "<name>"
cos:
  display_name: "<cos name>"
policies_repo: <value>
employer_rules: null
```

Add `  purpose: company` right after `  level:`, the `cos:` block right after the `ceo:` block, and `employer_rules: null` after `policies_repo:` (and after its block, if it has one).

`company/state.md`: frontmatter `framework_version: 0.1.1` becomes `framework_version: 0.2.0`.

`CLAUDE.md`: the intro line moves to the 0.2.0 form.

```
# before
You are the chief of staff (CoS) of **<org>** (id `<ID>`, level: <level>). You work for ...

# after
You are **<name>**, the chief of staff (CoS) of **<org>** (id `<ID>`, level: <level>, purpose: <purpose>). You work for ...
```

Keep the rest of the sentence. If the CEO customized this line, keep the customization and only add the name and the purpose.

Optional (cosmetic): `operating-model.md`, roles table, `| CoS |` becomes `| CoS (<name>) |`.

No change to `.claude/agents/*.md`: for purpose product, startup or company the account line renders exactly as in 0.1.1.

## Choosing job

Only if the CEO picks `job`. Apply these edits as well:

- `org.yaml`: `policy: null` (a pinned holding policy is removed: a job hq is never governed by a holding) and `employer_rules: "<pointer>"` or `null`.
- `CLAUDE.md`, "Account separation": replace the line "Never use other accounts (for example an employer's) or their connectors for this org's affairs, and never hand them to agents." with:

  ```
  - This hq supports the CEO's own work at an employer (purpose `job`). The employer's accounts and connectors used for this job are this org's accounts. Never use other accounts (for example the CEO's personal accounts) or their connectors for this org's affairs, and never hand them to agents. Where the hq remote lives (a personal or an employer account) is the CEO's choice and does not make other personal accounts usable.
  - The employer's rules on data, AI tools and accounts come first. Pointer: `employer_rules` in `org.yaml` (`null` when none was given). When a request touches employer data, AI tool use, accounts or where hq is pushed, re-read it and follow the stricter rule. Keep in hq only what those rules allow.
  - This hq is never registered in a holding portfolio and pins no holding policy.
  ```

- `CLAUDE.md`: remove the whole "Escalation to the holding" section.
- `company/decision-queue.md`: the card skeleton loses the `- escalate: no` line, and every open card loses its `escalate:` line. For cards that had `escalate: holding`, also remove the ` [escalate: holding]` marker from "Open decisions" in `company/state.md`, and tell the CEO that the holding no longer sees them.
- Each catalog department `.claude/agents/<id>.md`: in the "Account separation" line, replace `(for example an employer's)` with `(for example the CEO's personal accounts; the employer's accounts used for this job are this org's accounts)`.
- If a holding lists this hq in `portfolio.yaml`, the CEO removes the entry there (a commit in the holding hq, made by the holding CoS).

## Readers during the transition

An `org/1` `org.yaml` is read as purpose `company` with the CoS name unset. `portfolio-brief` relies on this, so hqs not yet migrated keep working.

## Check after applying

- `org.yaml` parses and follows `schemas/org.yaml` (`org/2`).
- `framework_version` is equal in `org.yaml` and in `company/state.md`.
- `grep -rn "org/1" org.yaml` finds nothing.
