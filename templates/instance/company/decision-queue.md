# Decision queue

Open decisions for the CEO. Answer in chat; the CoS records the answer with `record-decision` and removes the card. Each card has exactly one of `proposed_by: <dept-id|cos>` or `needs analysis by: <dept-id>`. Full schema: `schemas/decision-card.md` in the ceo-of-agents plugin.
